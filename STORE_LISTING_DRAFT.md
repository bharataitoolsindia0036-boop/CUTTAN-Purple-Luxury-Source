# CUTTAN — store listing draft (review before publishing)

**Short description:** Local-first video colour, trim and export tools.

**Long description:** CUTTAN is a mobile-first video editing starter with local video preview, colour presets, trim controls, aspect-ratio output, rotation, playback speed and optional replacement audio. Video rendering is performed locally in the browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly where the device and browser support it. Large videos may fail or run slowly on low-memory devices.

**Do not claim yet:** ten-layer editing, text/sticker burn-in, audio mixing, transitions, AI tools, guaranteed 4K, or any feature marked not implemented in `README.md` and `AUDIT_REPORT.md`.
